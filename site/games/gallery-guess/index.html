How joining works

One player creates a room and shows an invite code. You scan it with your phone camera, type your name and get a reply code. The host scans your reply, and you're in. Anyone already in the room can invite the next player.

Works best on the same Wi-Fi. Across the internet it usually works too; some strict networks (offices, hotels, some mobile carriers) block direct connections.
